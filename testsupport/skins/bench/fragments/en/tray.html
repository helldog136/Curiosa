Your cookies

This site uses very few cookies. Here is what they are for.

Always on, necessary

Your language, to show the site in the language you chose. If you decline, that choice is also remembered for 30 days so you are not asked again.

With your consent

The date of your last visit, kept in your browser only to show you what is new since you last came.

Nothing is sent anywhere else: no tracking, no advertising, no third-party service. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
