Privacy policy

This page describes what the site "My site" records about you. It is generated automatically from how the site really works.

Who is responsible?

The site "My site" is run by its owner. For any question about your data, write to contact@example.org.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • Language: the site remembers the language you choose (cookie "curiosa_locale", 1 year). It is needed to show you the site in the right language.
  • News: only if you click "Accept cookies" in the site's banner, the site remembers in your browser the date of your last visit to flag what is new since you last came (cookies "curiosa_news", "curiosa_seen" and "curiosa_since", up to 1 year). Nothing is sent anywhere else. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookies" link in the footer: everything is then erased.
  • Your answer: if you answer "No thanks" to the cookie banner, the site remembers that refusal (cookie "curiosa_cookies", 30 days) so it does not ask again on every page. If you close the banner with the Escape key, that marker only lasts for your visit. It only holds a plain "no" or "later".
  • Light or dark: when the site offers both looks and you pick one with the switch in the header, that choice is kept in your browser only (local storage "curiosa-mode"). It is not a cookie: it is never sent to the site or to anyone, and it only serves to show the pages the way you asked. Without a choice from you, the site follows your device setting.
  • The site sets no advertising or tracking cookie and uses no third-party measurement service.

Audience measurement

The site does not count its visits.

Your rights

You can ask for access to the data about you, for its correction or erasure, object to its processing, or lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
